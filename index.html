Demo 1: The Resonator

Pulse the chamber and watch the gas. The shading is local pressure, warm where the gas is compressed and cool where it is rarefied. The dark dots are gas parcels carried by the wave. The green dot is a pressure sensor in the closed end wall; the graph below is its reading over time. It rings, and it always fades.

The chamber is a resonator: pulsed, it rings at its own pitch and fades. Something must pump it for a tone to last.
